# Diary — 2026-10-20 (09)

## Formal verification of the shader mathematics (ADR-0006)

The owner asked for the mathematics and optics to be machine-checked in tests or hooks. What was built and what was not.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Z3 can decide the claims the shaders rely on | falloff polynomials, SDF sign, Snell, Fresnel as SMT-LIB over real arithmetic | **CONFIRMED**: all decided in under 0.3 s total |
| The rounded-rect SDF is 1-Lipschitz | one query, 60 s | **UNKNOWN** (not a proof) |
| ... after splitting into 64 sign cases | 25 s per case, 8 in parallel | **PARTLY**: 48 unsat, 16 timeout; the 16 are the cases where both points are in a corner region, which also need the abs step as a lemma. **Stays open.** |

An earlier attempt at the case split ran 64 cases in the foreground with no progress output and was cut off by the tool
timeout, producing no result. The rerun logged per case in the background.

Five proofs now decide exactly as declared: falloff-curves (20 answers), rounded-rect-sdf (13), snell-refraction (4),
schlick-fresnel (7), sellmeier-dispersion (23). The SDF proof contains the corner-wedge theorem and the outermost-row theorem,
and its last control shows that WITHOUT the half-pixel bias the outer row of a pointy shape is not covered: the
defect found and fixed in diary 2026-10-20-08, stated mathematically.

The Sellmeier data as typed in the code reproduces the published indices: N-BK7 n_e 1.51872, water 1.33483 at 546.1 nm,
diamond 2.42359, and no resonance of any of the five materials lies in the visible band, which is what makes normal
dispersion provable.

Harness tests also cover: a false lemma is refuted (sat), a missing solver is an error and never an empty success, a call
is not mistaken for a definition, comments and spacing do not change a digest but an operator does, stale pins are
rewritten without touching anything else.

## Limits stated in the record

Real-number models only; float32 and half rounding are outside the proofs. Native Liquid Glass parity is measured, not proved.
Z3 is a development dependency; a machine without it fails loudly. A pre-push hook is the owner's decision (repository trust).
