## Mixed sharp/rounded corners are a real missing branch

Continuing the complete outstanding roadmap, not only appearance tuning. Independent read-only audits were run for capture/
private-API work (Claude) and proof obligations (Codex); their recommendations remain hypotheses until checked locally.

The analytic field bypassed radius division only when **all four** radii were zero. With a zero bottom-right radius among
rounded corners, the initial candidate became non-finite; with another sharp corner, its invalid candidate was skipped and
outside-corner distances were wrong by up to 3.62 px in the probe. A radius-zero corner now evaluates the box branch before
any division. Normal construction also tests squared length before reciprocal square root, rather than manufacturing NaNs
and relying on a later comparison to select an axis.

Regression: `mixed sharp and rounded corners keep finite distances and unit gradients` was red on six circular/continuous
mixtures, then green. It checks a grid plus subpixel probes in every quadrant, unit directions, and an independent circular
per-corner formula. The existing five-case native-reference comparison remains green; the new sharp-corner behavior does
not claim Apple's zero-radius division is defined. `nonfinite corner radii become sharp finite corners` was red for NaN/
infinity and passes after finite-value resolution.

The scheduler's Intel architecture inheritance exposed an existing test-build failure: the optional benchmark uploaded
Swift `Float16` values, unavailable on Intel. It now uploads IEEE binary16 `UInt16` payloads using the existing conversion.
Native Apple-silicon jobs are explicitly launched through `arch -arm64`; there was no shader/Metal compiler failure.
