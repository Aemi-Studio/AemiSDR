# Diary — 2026-10-20 (15)

## Edge-local checks disproved the old rim model and capsule field

The owner asked for dots/lines at arbitrary angles, not only 0/45/90 degrees or a whole-image score. The report now separates
0–5, 5–15 and 15–40 px depth bands, both capsule ends and both circle halves at 17/30/63/77 degrees, retaining top/bottom
asymmetry. Labelled native/custom/4x-error crops were generated, opened in Preview and inspected.

### Correct the instrument before tuning the renderer

The former decoder inverted the veil but not the neutral rim light. A synthetic known warp crossing two phase periods,
with a neutral light added, failed by 5.22 px at the 99th percentile. Subtracting the flat red channel from sine/cosine
channels cancels both neutral additions. Clipped/weak codes are excluded from scores, but clipped strips remain connected
while unwrapping; otherwise disconnected islands acquire arbitrary whole-period offsets. Coordinates decoded from the lab
are image indices (i); shape fields must be evaluated at pixel centres (i+0.5).

The circular-capsule control also exposed a mismatch: our renderer used circular ends while the report used a continuous
field. Initially suspected to be just a report error, this proved to be a **renderer error**: the native capsule follows the
continuous field. Without refitting the pull, that field reduced native pull error near 77 degrees from roughly 9–11 px to
below a pixel. Rendering the existing continuous rounded rectangle at the same capsule size confirmed the prediction on
both dots and grid. `.capsule` now supplies continuous radii; a circular capsule remains expressible with circular corners.

### Native rim: circular arc, not exponential/Gaussian

QuartzCore AIR `glass_background_r_lpf` loads `inner_refraction_amount` (uniform byte 16) and
`inner_refraction_inv_height` (byte 20), then evaluates:

    t = saturate(depth * inverseHeight)
    pull = amount * (1 - sqrt(t * (2 - t)))

This is static functional evidence, not an observation of CPU-populated uniforms. The captures infer **60 pt amplitude and
20 pt height**. At 3x, selected circle measurements / predictions (px): 0.5 deep 156.77/156.81, 1.5 deep 139.91/140.00,
2.5 deep 128.66/128.58, 8.5 deep 87.70/87.64, 20.5 deep 44.49/44.51, 50.5 deep 2.28/2.27. The former fit under-pulled by
10.99 px at the first half-pixel. The native preset now uses the arc; exponential/Gaussian remains a configurable profile.

## Blur controls and rejected optimisation

The blur now supports one to three isotropic Gaussian components, separately normalised edge/interior weights and a depth
ramp. Presets: native approximation, Gaussian, heavy-tailed three-Gaussian `optical` (not a proved physical PSF), frosted.
Veil colour/removal and optional per-channel fringing are exposed; native-parity defaults keep fringing off. Native-like
appearance is not the same objective as the owner's preferred clearer-edge/blurrier-interior appearance.

A merged vertical-blur/glass experiment failed displaced pixel equivalence by **5/255**: paired bilinear taps and fractional
lookup do not commute. Removed, rather than shipping an unproved speed claim. The ordinary paired two-pass convolution stays.
Current whole-pipeline GPU timing on M2 Pro: 300x200 pt at 3x 0.465 ms; 650x650 pt at 3x 2.362 ms. Plain/paired ratios
1.269 and 1.370, duplicated-paired control ratios 0.996 and 1.001. This is not native-background timing or a frame guarantee.

Triangle base-corner dark wedges were another real defect: the inward pull can look beyond the shape's bounding box, where
blur scissors had left cleared/unwritten texels. The computed region now covers displaced samples plus convolution support,
including chromatic extremes. GPU uniform-grey reproduction failed by 128/255 without sufficient reach, then passed.
Fresh simulator grey-triangle captures had zero dark interior pixels in both native and custom glass; the corrected bottom
vertex comparison was opened as well. The wider/brighter custom rim-light tail remains visible.

## Verification and limits

Red-first regressions:

- `the native rim profile follows the measured displacement to within a quarter pixel`: former fit fails by 10.99 px.
- `the shader pulls by the same amount as the rim function`: missing arc branch fails by 26.82 px.
- `capsule optics use the native continuous field near off-axis joins`: circular field fails by 255/255.
- `triangle corners preserve a uniform backdrop when the rim looks beyond the shape bounds`: insufficient reach, including
  the 0.9 chromatic case, fails by 128/255.
- `paired blur taps preserve displaced textured sampling at fractional positions`: merged experiment fails by 5/255.
- `mutating blur components keeps the active weights normalised`: empty/four-component/overflowing weights fail before repair.
- `invalid and enormous blur radii produce finite bounded kernels`: NaN previously traps during integer conversion; clamp
  before conversion and treat non-finite sigma as effectively identity.
