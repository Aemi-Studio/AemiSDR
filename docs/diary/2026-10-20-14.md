# Diary — 2026-10-20 (14)

## Measuring the deformation itself, and the direction of the edge

SSIM mixes deformation, blur and tone, so when it moved I could not say which one changed. A deformation diff isolates the
geometry: the lab background carries a position code (green and blue are the sine and cosine of the x, or y, position, period
48 px), whose phase survives a Gaussian blur and an additive lift. Inverting the tone and unwrapping the phase from the
undistorted interior gives, for every pixel of the glass, the position it samples. Comparing the maps of native glass and ours
compares the deformation alone; the same map gives the native rim profile exactly.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Our rim is the native rim | endpoint error of the sampling maps | **REFUTED**: 10.9 px mean error at 5 to 15 px in, 39 px in the first 5 (square); beyond 40 px under 0.5 |
| The rim depends on the shape | the profile of five shapes | **REFUTED**: identical to 1 or 2 px for square, capsule, circle, rounded rectangle and triangle; purely inward (tangential 0) |
| The rim is a Gaussian | five families fitted to the measured profile | **REFUTED**: Gaussian rms 6.3 px, max 25; power law 1.3; bevel refraction 2.5; two-term (exponential spike + Gaussian body) **0.62 px, max 1.5** |
| Triangle banding comes from the gradient | raster distance transform against exact segments | **CONFIRMED**: a finite difference of a half-pixel field steps along a slanted edge; the exact field has one gradient (test: raster 3x worse) |
| Native glass has chromatic aberration | static: Apple's shaders; runtime: gray-coded background | **Code exists** (`aberration_*` in all variants); **not active** for clear (channels equal to 0.07 of 255 at every depth) |
| The dark ring is the same all round | polar profile of a circle over grey | **REFUTED**: it follows n.x^2, full on the left and right edges, absent on top and bottom |
| There is a bright inner rim | same | **CONFIRMED**: strongest on top and bottom edges (|n.y|^1.4 sharp, |n.y|^10 tail), absent on the sides, 1.4 x per unit of luma |
| Dark appearance changes clear glass | flat colours in dark | **REFUTED**: identical |
| The user's observation of the ring and rim light | polar measurement | **CONFIRMED** (the ring is invisible on the horizontal edges; the light is brightest there) |

Results: deformation similarity for the square 84.6 to 93.8, circle 92.1, rounded rectangle 93.6, triangle 89.5, capsule 86.1;
mean endpoint error 1.98 px to 0.45 (square). Edge error across a circle (levels of 255, all angles): over black 6.8 to 2.3,
over grey 13.5 to 3.1, over white 3.6 to 0.7. Similarity against native clear glass over 8 shapes and 4 backgrounds: mean
92.8 to **97.3**, min 88.1 to 92.5, max 99.3 (the complex pattern is 98.3 to 99.3 for every shape).
