# 2026-11-13-01: the variable blur's edges (Sunset's "no clipping where there is blur")

- Over stripes in Sunset, the blur under the window's address bar stopped on a hard line: the stripes came back sharp at once.
- Cause 1, the mask was not fitted. The filters were assigned while the SwiftUI-hosted layer was still empty (0×0); the
  variableBlur's mask is fitted to the layer's size at assignment, so it never fitted, and the whole band blurred at full
  strength up to its edge. Changing the mask's shape changed no pixel, which gave it away. Fix: `BlurBackdropLayer.refit()`,
  called by `BlurBackdropNSView.layout()` when the size changes (variable blurs only).
- Cause 2, the straight fall. The alpha scales the radius, and fine detail is gone at a small part of the radius, so content
  stays blurred out until the last points of the fall. Now cubic: half way through the fall, an eighth of the radius.
  Probe (stripes and text, 150×240 backdrops, radius 20): row-band standard deviation over the fall, straight 0 0 0 0 0 0 1 4 32,
  cubic 0 0 0 1 3 24 41 40 49 (the pattern comes back over the fall instead of at its end).
- Tests: `refit` assigns a variable blur again and leaves a plain one; the cubic's midpoint. 28 blur tests pass.

## Masks with two directions (for Sunset's find bar)

- `BlurMask.around(width:height:fade:)`: full in the middle, falling towards every side (product of the two falls). Tried as the
  find bar's halo: the owner rejected it (a fuzzy frame).
- `BlurMask.corner(_:width:height:fade:)`: full from a corner along its two sides, falling towards the two others. The find bar's
  blur reaches its pane's corner and fades into the page.
- Both images are 128×128 (edge masks stay 4 wide). Tests: both masks' corners and middles; 30 blur tests pass.
