# Diary — 2026-10-22 (09)

## Clear glass live backdrop capture and SwiftUI integration

Following the delivery of the clear glass renderer, still-image UIKit view, and image renderer, clear glass was
integrated with the live backdrop capture architecture and exposed to SwiftUI.

### Live backdrop texture leasing in ClearGlassUIView

- Extended `ClearGlassUIView` with `setSourceTexture(_:origin:onConsumed:)`.
- Wrapped textures in `TextureSourceLease` so that source slots remain held until the view replaces the source or is
  destroyed AND all in-flight Metal command buffers finish executing on the GPU.
- Added `onReadyForFirstCapture` and `onContentHierarchyChanged` lifecycle callbacks, allowing the view to trigger
  initial captures and invalidate lookup caches without arbitrary timer delays.

### SwiftUI clear glass overlay and View modifier

- Created `_ClearGlassOverlay` (`UIViewRepresentable`), wiring `ClearGlassUIView` with `BackdropCaptureCoordinator`.
- Introduced `ClearGlassView` for container-adaptive background overlays with continuous capture and custom refresh rates.
- Added `.clearGlass(...)` view modifier.
