# Diary — 2026-10-20 (12)

## Performance: a harness that checks itself, then one change that earned its place

Scheduled as step 5 of roadmap 0019 and brought forward on request. The method came first, because GPU timing is easy to get wrong.

### Method

| Attempt | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Earlier lens timing, 40 repeated draws, no blending | every variant read about 0.016 ms | **REJECTED**: launch overhead and overdraw removal, not shading |
| Blending on, constant-colour floor pass, linearity checks | 8 passes 0.836 ms/pass, 32 passes 0.876 (+5%); 1024^2 0.222 against 0.876 at 2048^2 (3.95x for 4x area) | **ACCEPTED**: cost is linear in passes and area |
| Baseline and working tree, same source, tree always second | differed by up to 24% | **REJECTED**: order and clock-ramp bias, and no noise estimate |
| Rotating order, plus a control (the baseline compiled twice) in every run | control within 0.96 to 1.08 at full coverage, about 2% at typical | **ACCEPTED**: effects under about 8% cannot be shown by timing |

A change is kept only if it preserves every pixel (`LensBaselineEquivalenceTests`, frozen baseline in
`Verification/Reference/LiquidLensBaseline.metal`, which is itself shown to fail on a changed weight) and clears the control's spread across repeated runs.

### Apple's shader and our port (ms per 2048x2048 pass, bgra8, blending, same GPU)

constant-colour floor 0.026; Apple `glass_foreground_sdf` float 0.860, half 0.902; our reference port 0.899 (+4%, inside the noise).
The port costs what Apple's shader costs. This compares one pass of Apple's against our port of the same pass, not our lens.

### Our lens (baseline, full coverage, ms per 2048x2048 pass)

monochrome 0.17, default chromatic 0.27 to 0.29, fresnel 0.29, aspheric 0.28, high-fidelity refraction 0.37 to 0.39, thick lens 0.47, spectral 0.55 to 0.61.
Two extra texture samples cost about 0.10 ms (monochrome to chromatic), so sampling is the largest single cost. The numbers at "typical"
coverage are unscissored and overstate real cost: the renderer already scissors to the lens bounds in overlay mode.

### Changes tried

| Change | Pixels | Timing | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spectral: reuse the middle-wavelength sample for alpha (a sixth sample sat at an identical coordinate) | identical, 0/255 | spectral full coverage x0.93, x0.96, x0.93 against control x1.01, x1.04, x1.04: about 8 to 10% faster | **yes** |
| Single-surface refraction in closed form (shares sin^2 theta, no 3-vector) | within 1/255 | high-fidelity x0.99, x0.99, x0.97 against control 0.97 to 1.08: nothing measurable | **no, reverted** |

Refraction arithmetic is not the bottleneck. The remaining cost is texture sampling and fixed per-fragment work.

### Not done

not decoded yet, and it depends on the clear-glass optics (roadmap 0019 step 3). Likely next wins are structural: fewer samples
(chromatic fringes from one sample plus a cheap shift, or a lower-resolution fringe), not ALU.
