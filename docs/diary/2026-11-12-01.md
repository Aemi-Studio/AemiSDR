## Probes for roadmap 0020 (macOS 27.0.1, Swift 6.4.2, no window opened unless said)

Script `swift` on the runtime (`objc_lookUpClass`, `class_getImageName`, `method_getTypeEncoding`):

- `CAFilter`, `CABackdropLayer`: image QuartzCore.
- `+[CAFilter filterWithType:]` `@24@0:8@16` (object in, object out); `-[CAFilter inputKeys]` `@16@0:8`;
  `+[CAFilter filterTypes]` lists `gaussianBlur` and `variableBlur`.
- `CABackdropLayer`: `-setScale:` `v24@0:8d16`, `-setGroupName:` `v24@0:8@16`, `-setWindowServerAware:` `v20@0:8B16`.
- `variableBlur` inputs: `inputMaskImage`, `inputRadius`, `inputNormalizeEdges`, `inputNormalizeEdgesTransparent`, `inputFade`,
  `inputDither`, `inputSourceSublayerName`, `inputBlurFill…`. `colorSaturate`, `colorBrightness`: `inputAmount`.
- `setValue(3, forKey: "inputNoSuchKey")` on a `CAFilter`: no exception, reads back 3. Unknown keys are silently kept, so
  the key must be checked against `inputKeys` before it is written.

In Sunset (a throw-away copy, driven in the background by its `axdrive`; picture in Sunset's `docs/research/blur`):
a `CABackdropLayer` 76 pt tall over a web page, filters `gaussianBlur` 24, `variableBlur` 24 with a 4 × 128 mask (opaque over the
top half, falling to clear over the bottom half), and each with `colorSaturate` 1.8 + `colorBrightness` -0.08. Verdicts:
the variable blur's radius falls to nothing where the mask does (no hard line, no double image); the mask image is stretched to the
layer (a 128-pixel mask on a 152-pixel layer faded over the bottom half); masking a uniform blur's opacity instead shows the sharp
page through at half strength; `inputNormalizeEdges` leaves a faint light band at the bottom over a white page.
